## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Tetherloom: Orbit Run is a minimalist one-tap arcade game.

Your Lumen, a small orb of light, orbits a glowing anchor. Tap the screen and the Lumen
detaches, flying off along its orbit's tangent. Reach the next anchor to get captured and
keep climbing. Miss, fall below the screen, and the run is over.

How to play:
• One tap: launch the Lumen from its orbit
• Capture anchors to score points
• Chain captures to build your combo and score multiplier
• Beat your own high score

Features:
• Simple controls that are easy to learn and hard to master
• Short runs that fit any break
• Fully offline, no account, no ads
• Requires no permissions
• Progress stats: best score and best combo
• Settings for haptics, screen shake, and reduced effects
• English and Polish language support

More modes, Daily Pattern and Practice, are coming in future updates.
